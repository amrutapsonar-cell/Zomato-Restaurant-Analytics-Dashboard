An interactive **Power BI Dashboard** analyzing **51,717 restaurant listings** across Bengaluru to uncover actionable insights into market density, pricing trends, online delivery adoption, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 📊 Executive Summary & Key Insights

### 1. Market Volume & Geographic Distribution
* **Top Hubs:** **BTM Layout** is the largest market hub with **~5.12K outlets (9.91%)**, followed by **HSR Layout (~2.52K)** and **Koramangala 5th Block (~2.50K)**.
* **Secondary Hubs:** **JP Nagar (~2.24K)**, **Whitefield (~2.14K)**, and **Indiranagar (~2.08K)** maintain strong market representation.

### 2. Restaurant Formats & Cuisines
* **Dominant Categories:** **Quick Bites** account for the largest proportion of outlets at **36.99% (~19.13K)**, followed by **Casual Dining at 19.97% (~10.33K)** and **Cafes at 7.22% (~3.73K)**.
* **Service Types:** Delivery-focused models and Dessert Parlors comprise **5.04%** and **4.38%** of the market, respectively.
* **Popular Cuisines:** North Indian, Chinese, South Indian, Biriyani, and Fast Food dominate across major hubs.

### 3. Customer Satisfaction & Ratings Breakdown
Across **51.72K cataloged listings**, customer ratings are distributed as follows:
* 🟦 **Average (3.0 – 3.9):** 52.22% (27.01K outlets)
* 🟪 **Good (4.0 – 4.4):** 21.57% (11.16K outlets)
* 🟦 **Poor (< 3.0):** 19.53% (10.10K outlets)
* 🟧 **Unrated:** 4.27% (2.21K outlets)
* 🟥 **Excellent (4.5 – 5.0):** 2.41% (1.24K outlets)

### 4. Top-Performing Chain Brands
High-performing chain brands demonstrate strong ratings across multiple outlets and reviews:
* **Onesta:** Average rating of **4.41** (85 votes/reviews analyzed)
* **Empire Restaurant:** Average rating of **4.03** (71 votes/reviews analyzed)
* **Kanti Sweets:** Average rating of **3.90** (68 votes/reviews analyzed)
* **Polar Bear:** Average rating of **3.77** (65 votes/reviews analyzed)

---

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Target Growth in High-Density Hubs:** Concentrate expansion and delivery network optimization in **BTM Layout, HSR, and Koramangala**, which together drive the largest share of order traffic and listing volume.
2. **Capitalize on Quick Bites & Online Delivery:** Given that Quick Bites and Casual Dining represent over **56%** of restaurant types, operational focus should prioritize rapid service execution and optimized online delivery channels.
3. **Elevate Mid-Tier Ratings:** With **~52% of outlets** sitting in the `3.0–3.9` rating bracket, implementing quality assurance, better packaging, and customer feedback loops presents a clear opportunity to push outlets into the `4.0+` tier.
